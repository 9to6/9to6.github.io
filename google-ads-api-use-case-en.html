Google Ads API Use Case

How UniqBuild uses Google Ads and the Google Ads API

About UniqBuild

UniqBuild develops and operates mobile applications. We are based in Seoul, Republic of Korea, and publish iOS and Android apps under our own developer accounts on the App Store and Google Play.

Use of Google Ads

We use Google Ads to promote our own mobile apps and acquire app users, primarily through Google Ads app install campaigns. All Google Ads accounts referenced in our use of the API are owned and operated by UniqBuild.

Use of the Google Ads API

The Google Ads API is used internally to retrieve campaign performance data from our own Google Ads accounts, including cost, impressions, clicks, and app install conversion metrics.

The data is used for internal reporting and analysis, including dashboards or reports used by UniqBuild, to monitor campaign performance and compare efficiency across our owned apps.

Representative fields and metrics we read from the API:

  • campaign.name, campaign.status — campaign identification and state
  • metrics.cost_micros — ad spend
  • metrics.impressions, metrics.clicks — reach and engagement
  • metrics.conversions — app install conversions
  • Derived metrics computed internally: CPI (cost per install), CPC, install conversion rate, and ROAS

Data flow: Google Ads API → UniqBuild internal data store → internal dashboard viewed by UniqBuild staff only. No data is exposed to or shared with external parties.

Scope Limitation

We do not provide advertising agency services, manage third-party Google Ads accounts, or process Google Ads data on behalf of external customers. The API is used only to retrieve and analyze performance data for our own app promotion campaigns.
