Advertising & Marketing

UniqBuild runs Google Ads app install campaigns to acquire users for our own mobile apps, such as WallFit, BottleLog, and KeyQuest Typing. The Google Ads API is used to retrieve campaign performance data (cost, impressions, clicks, and app install conversion metrics) from our own Google Ads accounts for internal reporting and analysis only. We do not provide advertising agency services, manage third-party Google Ads accounts, or process Google Ads data on behalf of external customers.
